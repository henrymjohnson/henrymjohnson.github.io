Economics of Pricing Craftwork

A syllabus draft, written with Claude, for a reading course on how craftspeople (paperhangers in particular) price their work: eight units in four parts over 36 weeks of reading, then a writing phase. Each week has required readings, and podcasts marked High (listen first) or Low (fine to skip). Tick what you finish and answer the questions as you go; it all stays in your own browser.
